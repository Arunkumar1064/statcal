Tips for Land & Area Measurements

Measuring Irregular Fields

For irregular fields, divide the area into regular shapes (rectangles, triangles) and add the individual areas together for the total.

Slope Guidelines for Irrigation
  • 0-3%: Suitable for most irrigation methods
  • 3-5%: Suitable for sprinkler systems
  • 5-8%: May require terracing or specialized irrigation
  • 8-15%: Challenging for irrigation, requires special planning
  • 15%+: Not recommended for conventional irrigation
