Planting Guidelines

Recommended Seeding Rates
Crop Seeding Rate (kg/ha) Plant Population (plants/ha)
Wheat 80-120 2,000,000-3,500,000
Corn/Maize 20-30 60,000-90,000

Factors Affecting Seed Requirements
  • Germination Rate: Lower germination requires higher seeding rates
  • Soil Conditions: Poor soils may require more seeds
  • Planting Method: Broadcast seeding requires more seeds than drilling
  • Planting Time: Late plantings often require higher rates
  • Seed Size: Varies by variety and affects the number of seeds per kg
Germination Quality Guide
  • Excellent: >90% germination
  • Good: 75-90% germination
  • Fair: 60-75% germination
  • Poor: <60% germination - consider obtaining new seed
