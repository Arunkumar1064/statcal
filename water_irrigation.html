Water Conservation Strategies
  • Schedule irrigation early morning or late evening to reduce evaporation losses
  • Use soil moisture sensors to determine when irrigation is truly needed
  • Implement deficit irrigation during non-critical growth stages
  • Apply mulch to reduce soil surface evaporation
  • Harvest rainwater for supplemental irrigation when possible
  • Maintain irrigation systems to prevent leaks and ensure uniform application
